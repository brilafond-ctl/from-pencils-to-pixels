From Pencils to Pixels

Follow two writing technologies through Baron’s argument. Notice where familiar stories about new tools hold true and where they might need revising.

    01 / Set Out

    Start Your Report

    Read Dennis Baron’s “From Pencils to Pixels: The Stages of Literacy Technology” alongside this activity. Allow about 20–30 minutes for this activity after reading.

    Why this reading?

    Every new writing technology (e.g., the pencil, the typewriter, the computer) has faced initial skepticism about its authenticity, trust, and impact on communication. In this 1999 essay, linguist Dennis Baron traces how literacy technologies evolve through predictable stages of adaptation, resistance, and normalization, helping us contextualize current anxieties surrounding generative AI and digital composition.
